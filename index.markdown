Hi there! I'm Sam, a Clinical Assistant Professor of Data Science at NYU. I love teaching, and as a clinical professor, most of my academic energy is devoted towards becoming a better teacher for courses in machine learning, artificial intelligence, and mathematics.

I did my PhD up the street in the [Theory of Computation Group](https://theory.cs.columbia.edu/) at [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu/), where I was extremely fortunate to be advised by [Daniel Hsu](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~djhsu/) and [Jeannette Wing](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~wing/). I was also an undergraduate at Columbia, where I double-majored in computer science and philosophy. For philosophy, I worked with the inimitable [Achille Varzi](https://www.columbia.edu/~av72/), who advised my senior thesis on machine learning fairness from a philosophy of science perspective. He [irrevocably changed my view on donuts](https://youtu.be/2nu-7asEy0U?si=gTmkjrGWepHCQhwq).

I also like running, nerding out about books and [mo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FWG3Dfss3Jc)[vi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2VeLN3IDjzQ)[es](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rph_1DODXDU)[,](/assets/vig.JPG) and a good magic trick.
